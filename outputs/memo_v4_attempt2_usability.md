## The answer in one sentence

**Prioritise usability next quarter, starting with ad interruptions, shuffle and playback controls, and queue and playlist management.**

## What we looked at

- The source set covers reviews from May 2022 to November 2023: **660,622** reviews analysed [A2c], including **660,555** completed classified reviews [A1].
- The issue ranking covers **293,810** complaint or cancellation reviews [A2] across **39** issues [A2b].
- Reviews were classified by topic, intent and seriousness; priority score means complaint count multiplied by average seriousness on a **1-5** scale [A10].
- The four options below compare area-level complaints, average seriousness, priority score and the Aug-Oct trend.

## Why this area wins

1. **Usability leads among the four options by area priority score:** **180,799** [A32]. However, the highest-ranked individual issue is `ISS-other-general_negative` (General Negative Feedback), with **79,391** complaints [C001], average seriousness **1.99** [C002], and a priority score of **157,846** [C003]. That broad “other” issue is not one of the four choices, and its lower seriousness suggests less specific guidance for a product effort.
2. **The trend supports acting on usability at the end of the review window.** Usability complaints were **11.1%** of **85,236** classified reviews in Aug-Oct 2022 [A14] and **12.4%** of **161,173** in Aug-Oct 2023 [A23]. These are shares in self-selected reviews, not evidence that usability problems caused a change.
3. **Start with the most actionable usability issues.** `ISS-usability-ads_interruptions` (Frequent Ad Interruptions) has **36,257** complaints [C007] and a **77,273** priority score [C009]. `ISS-usability-controls_shuffle_skip` (Shuffle and Playback Controls) has **14,857** complaints [C010] and a **44,091** priority score [C012]. `ISS-usability-queue_playlist_management` (Queue and Playlist Management) has **8,507** complaints [C022] and a **25,589** priority score [C024]. Customers describe disruptive ads, unwanted shuffle, and difficulty playing or managing their own playlists. First, review these flows together and identify changes that restore clear control of listening.

## Why not the others?

- **Billing/support:** Its area score is close, but lower than usability; its recent complaint share is notable. `ISS-billing-free_tier_restrictions` (Free-Tier Feature Restrictions) is a major issue, with **48,031** complaints [C004] and a **140,647** priority score [C006]. Revisit the choice if the team can address the restrictions without weakening the intended free-tier experience.
- **Playback:** The area has higher average seriousness, **3.31** [A51], but its review share went from **7.5%** [A12] to **5.1%** [A21]. Keep reliability work visible; `ISS-playback-playback_stops_or_skips` (Playback Stops or Skips) scores **37,795** [C015].
- **Access:** Complaints are less numerous, though seriousness is high at **3.92** [A63]. `ISS-access-login_failure` (Unable to Log In) scores **28,532** [C018]; address severe login failures as focused reliability work.

## What to keep in mind

These are historical public reviews written by self-selected reviewers, not a representative customer survey. Model labels have measured error: verifier agreement was **84.1%** for topic [A5], **91.4%** for intent [A6], and **88.4%** for exact severity [A7]. Cancellation language records stated intent, not observed churn. There is no revenue or plan data. **67** reviews were quarantined or unresolved [A3].
