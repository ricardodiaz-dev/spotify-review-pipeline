**Prioritise billing/support next quarter, starting with free-tier restrictions, because its recent complaint share is rising sharply even though usability has the highest overall product-area priority score.**

## Why this area wins

1. **The required ranking points to usability, but the latest trend changes the call.** The highest-ranked individual issue is `ISS-other-general_negative` (General Negative Feedback), with a priority score of 157,846 [C003]. Among product areas, usability ranks highest at 180,799 [A32]. Billing/support is close at 173,862 [A38], but its share moved from 4.0% [A16] to 21.4% [A25] of classified reviews in the comparison windows. For next quarter, that recent signal outweighs usability’s modestly higher overall score.
2. **The leading billing issue is specific and substantial.** `ISS-billing-free_tier_restrictions` (Free-Tier Feature Restrictions) has 48,031 complaints [C004], mean severity 2.93 [C005], and a priority score of 140,647 [C006]. This is the clearest first target within the recommended area.
3. **What customers are saying:** reviews describe losing song choice and controls under free-tier limits, including `84300454-6a70-40f5-89bc-f9818dd4076d` and `cba5ab8b-0bbb-45b8-8925-b6d843d12d42`. First, map which restrictions trigger these complaints and test clearer, more consistent free-tier controls and messaging.

## Why not the others?

- **Usability:** It wins on overall area priority score, and `ISS-usability-ads_interruptions` (Frequent Ad Interruptions) leads its listed issues at 36,257 complaints [C007] and priority score 77,273 [C009]. Still, its trend is steadier than billing/support’s sharp rise.
- **Playback:** It has high average seriousness, but its complaint share declined from 7.5% [A12] to 5.1% [A21]. `ISS-playback-playback_stops_or_skips` (Playback Stops or Skips) remains an important fix at priority score 37,795 [C015].
- **Access:** Complaints are serious on average, but the area has the lowest priority score of these options, 38,865 [A62]. Login failures merit attention, but the current evidence supports a narrower response than a quarter-wide priority.
